Industrial Maintenance AI Assistant — Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

OCP Group, Morocco · July 2026

During my internship, I developed a prototype intelligent assistant for industrial maintenance. It processes approved technical documentation and retrieves relevant information to generate grounded answers with source citations.

  • Designed the ingestion, chunking, embedding, retrieval and answer-generation pipeline.
  • Used local embeddings and vector storage for controlled retrieval of approved content.
  • Built a Streamlit interface for maintenance-related questions.
  • Implemented automated testing; 264 passing tests reported in the internship project.
  • Focused on source traceability, privacy and reducing unsupported responses.

Security learning roadmap

This site is being shaped as a professional cybersecurity portfolio, not only a resume. The goal is to show how I learn, document, and apply security concepts through projects, labs, notes, and technical evidence that can be reviewed by recruiters and hiring teams.

What This Portfolio Shows

A cybersecurity portfolio should demonstrate education, skills, knowledge, and genuine commitment to the field. It gives more depth than a one-page resume by connecting claims to actual work. The items below are planned evidence, not completed deliverables.

  • Hands-on evidence of security learning instead of only stated interests.
  • Documented projects, screenshots, and write-ups that explain how I approached problems.
  • Clear organization so recruiters can review technical growth quickly.

Why I Chose a Git-Based Portfolio

This portfolio uses a website and Git repositories to connect my learning to reviewable work. I chose a Git-based portfolio because it lets me publish with Markdown, connect directly to code repositories, and keep a visible record of iteration.
